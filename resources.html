MLAB journal club

Machine Learning to Accelerate Biology (MLAB) is our open seminar series. Speakers present statistical and machine learning methods that solve, or could solve, biological problems, from protein design and structure to single-cell, whole-genome modeling and viral evolution. Talks are informal and questions are encouraged.
