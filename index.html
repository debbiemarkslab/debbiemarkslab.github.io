Debora Marks Lab

Machine learning to accelerate biology

We build models to make sense of genetic variation and design biological tools for environmental and human health.

Research

Learning the language of life

Amino acids that mutate together across evolution tend to touch in 3D. We disentangle these couplings from sequence information to understand the structure and function of protein and RNA.

Genetic variation and protein design

Predicting the functional consequences of mutations, alone and in combination, to interpret human genetic variation and to design new sequences that stay functional and stable.

Viral evolution and immunology

Using machine learning models to predict viral escape/pathogenicity, improve immune recognition, and predict antibody targets.
